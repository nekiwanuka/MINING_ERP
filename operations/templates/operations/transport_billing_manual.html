Workflow Manual

Simplified transport billing

Create one transit, add customers by distance and rate per km, issue invoices, generate receipts for payments, then lock the trip when the delivery note is issued.

01Transit

Create trip, vehicle, route, and driver.

02Customers

Add cargo rows, distance, rate, and destination.

03Costs

Enter fuel, allowances, taxes, tolls, and border fees.

04Invoice

Generate pending invoices per customer.

05Issue

Approve and send the invoice.

06Receipt

Accept payment and generate a receipt.

07GR

Goods reached generates the delivery note and locks edits.

1

Transit details

Create one transport record for the vehicle trip. Enter the overall charge, vehicle, driver, origin, destination, and total route distance if known. The system can infer the common route and final distance from customer distances.

2

Customers

Add the customers and their delivery details. Enter each customer's total distance from the start of the transit and the customer's rate per km. Leave the manual agreed amount blank unless management wants to override the automatic calculation.

3

Distance sharing

If Martha is at 240 km, Peter is at 389 km, and Kresto is at 400 km, the system treats 240 km as the first branch-off because all customers share the route up to that distance.

4

In transit charges

Enter internal costs once. Use this section for fuel, driver allowances, turn-boy allowances, taxes, border fees, tolls, ferry fees, parking, and other trip deductions.

5

Internal cost assignment

Use General trip cost for shared trip deductions. Choose a specific customer only when that cost belongs to one customer's delivery for internal profit analysis. These costs stay hidden from customer invoices.

6

Generate customer invoices

Open the transport detail page and click Generate invoices. The system creates one pending customer invoice for each saved customer and opens the generated invoice or filtered invoice list.

7

Issue invoice

Open each pending invoice and click Issue after checking customer name, transit reference, route, goods, and amount. Issued invoices can be printed, downloaded, or sent to the customer.

8

Accept payment

After money is received, open the issued invoice and click Generate receipt. The system creates an official receipt, links it to the invoice, and moves the related trip to In Transit while the invoice remains issued.

9

Goods reached and delivery note

When the goods reach the customer, use GR - Goods Reached. The system marks the trip delivered and generates the delivery note. Receipt references are shown on the delivery note, and the trip is locked from further edits.

10

Fair sharing rule

The system divides the shared first leg equally across all customers, then adds the private distance after the branch-off for customers who travel farther.

11

Profit tracking

The transport detail page still keeps the accounting view. It compares customer invoice revenue against in transit charges and recorded expenses to show profit or loss.
